Frequently asked questions

What is an ATS and why does it matter?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) helps employers organise applications. Screening rules vary by employer. ResumeRadar checks recognised keywords and readable text; it cannot predict an employer’s decision.

How does ResumeRadar work?

Upload your CV (PDF or DOCX) and paste the job description you're applying to. ResumeRadar extracts keywords from both, estimates your keyword match, identifies missing keywords, checks ATS formatting, and gives AI-powered suggestions to make your experience clearer — with results usually ready within a minute.

Is ResumeRadar free?

Yes. The CV scan is completely free with no signup required. You get your keyword match estimate, missing keywords, formatting check, and AI suggestions at no cost.

What file formats are supported?

ResumeRadar supports PDF and DOCX files. For best ATS compatibility, we recommend submitting your CV as a PDF.

How do I improve my keyword match?

After scanning, look at the "Keywords Not Found" section — these are terms the job description mentions that aren't in your CV. Where you have that experience, add it to your skills section or experience bullets with a real example. Also check the AI suggestions for specific rewording tips.
