Privacy

{{ privacy_line }}

Your CV and the job description

  • If you upload a file, it is read once and deleted straight away. If you paste text, no file is created.
  • Your CV text and the job description are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to write your suggestions and, if you ask for one, your cover letter.
  • ResumeRadar does not save your CV text or add it to a database. The AI suggestions, cover letters, bullet rewrites and summaries are cached for up to one hour, so a repeat of the same request does not have to be written again. Our server logs never include your CV text or the AI reply.
  • Nothing is sold.

Your email address

  • Emailed report. If you ask for your report by email, we first send a short email with a link to confirm the address, and the report is sent only after you press Confirm. Both emails go through our email provider, Resend. ResumeRadar does not keep your address or add you to a mailing list. It keeps only a scrambled form of it, which cannot be turned back into the address, for up to 24 hours, to record that you confirmed and to limit how many confirmation emails one address can get.
  • Newsletter. Signing up to Shola's Tech Notes is optional. If you do, your first name and email address go to our newsletter provider, Beehiiv. You can unsubscribe from any issue.

What stays on your device

Your scan history is kept in your browser, not on our servers. Each entry holds the date, your score, the keyword counts, the first line of the job description (up to 60 characters) and a short code made from the job description, which lets ResumeRadar compare rescans for the same job. The code cannot be turned back into the job description. The "Clear history" button removes it all.

Your browser also remembers whether you have visited before, whether you signed up to the newsletter, and a random ID and a few settings for the current visit, which are cleared when you close the tab.

Feedback on a report

Under each report you can say whether it was useful and, if you like, add a comment. A Yes or No on its own is only added to a daily count. If you send a comment, we save only your comment, your Yes or No answer, whether you allow quoting and today's date, with nothing that links it to you or your scan, and delete it after 90 days. Web addresses, email addresses and phone numbers are removed before it is saved. A comment is only quoted on the site if you ticked the box to allow it, and never with your name.

Usage counts and limits

  • We count events such as scans started and completed, using a random ID for each visit. The counts are kept for up to 90 days and are not linked to your name, email address or CV.
  • To keep the tool free and stop abuse, your IP address is used to apply daily limits. Those counters expire within 24 hours.

Fonts

The site's fonts come from Google Fonts. When a page opens, your browser asks Google for them, which sends Google your IP address. Your CV, the job description and your email address are never sent to Google.

Who runs ResumeRadar

ResumeRadar is a free personal project built and run by Olushola Oladipupo. For any privacy question, message him on LinkedIn.
